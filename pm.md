**Helping digital agencies get organized, stay on track, and deliver results.**

Over the past several years, I’ve worked as a Project Manager for digital agencies, development firms, and creative teams across the US, Australia, and the Philippines. I specialize in organizing scattered projects, aligning remote teams, and building repeatable systems that save time, prevent errors, and drive results.

---

### My Role in Your Projects

Across every engagement, I’ve taken responsibility for:

- **Task breakdown and implementation**  
  Turning specs into clearly assigned, step-by-step tasks in tools like Asana and ClickUp.

- **Team coordination and documentation**  
  Making sure everyone knows what they need to do, when they need to do it, and how to get it done—using clear SOPs, screenshots, and screencasts.

- **Client communication**  
  Relaying progress and collecting feedback in a format that makes sense to clients, not just the team.

- **QA and final review**  
  Personally checking outputs before they go out, especially for visual, functional, and content accuracy.

- **Recruitment and onboarding**  
  Helping grow and train the team by sourcing candidates, screening applicants, and setting up systems for smooth onboarding.
